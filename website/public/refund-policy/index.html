Cancellation and refund policy

How to cancel and how refunds work.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for Surecast subscriptions sold by Exaltes Global Private Limited.

Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial with all features. You are not charged during the trial. If you cancel before it ends, you will not be charged at all.

Cancelling your subscription

  • You can cancel at any time from Settings → Billing in the app, or by emailing hello@surecast.live from your account email.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.
  • We confirm every cancellation by email.

Refunds

  • First payment: if you are not happy, ask for a refund within 7 days of your first paid charge and we will refund it in full.
  • Renewals: renewal payments are not refunded for partly used periods. If you forgot to cancel and have not used the Service since the renewal, write to us within 7 days of the charge and we will refund it.
  • Duplicate or incorrect charges: refunded in full once confirmed.
  • Service failure: if the Service is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours because of a fault on our side, you can ask for a pro-rata credit or refund for that time.
  • Plan downgrades take effect at the next billing date and do not create a refund for the current period.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@surecast.live with your account email, the date of the charge and the Razorpay payment ID if you have it. We reply within 2 business days.

Refund timelines

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days and returned to the original payment method through Razorpay. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show the credit.
