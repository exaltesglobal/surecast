The problem

Schedulers fail quietly. Your audience notices first.

Most tools treat publishing as a solved problem. Anyone running more than one brand knows it is not.

01

Silent failures

A token expires or a platform times out, and the post simply does not go out. Nobody is told until someone asks where the post went.

02

Pricing that punishes growth

Per-account and per-seat pricing means every new brand or client makes your tools more expensive, not your business more profitable.

03

No proof of work

When a client or manager asks what went out last month, you are stuck stitching together screenshots from five platforms.

Features

Built around one promise.

Everything a good scheduler does, plus a publishing engine that treats every post as something that has to land.

A workspace for every brand

Each brand gets its own workspace, social accounts, team access, calendar and reports. Nothing leaks between brands.

Automatic retry

Rate limits, timeouts and platform hiccups are retried with backoff. Every attempt is logged, so you can see exactly what happened.

Pre-flight checks

Fifteen minutes before each slot we check the token, the media and the text limits. Problems reach you before the post is due, and a watchdog flags any slot that was missed.

Token-expiry alerts

Connections that are about to expire are flagged days ahead, with a one-click reconnect. No more silent disconnects.

Failures in plain language

Instead of an error code you get the cause and the fix, for example: Instagram token expired, reconnect this channel.

One health dashboard

Posts out today, failures that need action and channels at risk, across every brand workspace, on one screen.

Approvals without seats

Send a link and your client, brand manager or boss approves or requests changes. No login, no extra seat, no extra cost.

Proof-of-publish reports

A monthly report per brand listing every post, when it went out and its live link. Share it or print it.

AI drafting and API

Draft and adapt captions per platform with AI, and plug Surecast into your own tools with the API and webhooks.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which platforms does Surecast support?

Bluesky, Mastodon and Telegram channels work today. LinkedIn, Facebook Pages, Instagram Business and X are built and are being switched on as each platform completes its app review. TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest and Google Business Profile are next.

What happens when a post fails?

Temporary errors such as rate limits or timeouts are retried automatically. If a post cannot go out, for example because a token expired, it stops at once and you get an email with the reason and the fix. The attempt log on the post shows every try.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with all features. You will not be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel at any time before that.

Do you charge per social account?

No. Each plan includes a fixed number of channels and unlimited posts. Approval links never use a seat.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from Billing in the app. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and does not renew. See our cancellation and refund policy for details.

Where is my data stored?

Your workspace data and media are stored on our servers and are never sold. You can export or delete your data at any time. Read the privacy policy for the full picture.

How do I pay?

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets through Razorpay. Prices are in Indian Rupees and GST is added at checkout where it applies.
