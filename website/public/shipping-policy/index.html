Shipping and delivery policy

Surecast is delivered online. Nothing is shipped.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Surecast is an online software service sold by Exaltes Global Private Limited. We do not sell or ship any physical products.

How the Service is delivered

  • After you sign up, your Surecast account is available immediately at app.surecast.live.
  • When you buy or upgrade a plan, the new plan is applied to your account as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a few minutes.
  • A confirmation and a GST invoice are sent to your account email.

Delivery charges

There are no shipping or delivery charges.

If your plan is not activated

If your payment succeeded but your plan has not changed within 2 hours, email support@surecast.live with your account email and Razorpay payment ID. We will activate it or refund the payment within 2 business days.
